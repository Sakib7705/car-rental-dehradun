Cancellation & Refund Policy

Flexible policies designed for smooth travel experiences in Uttarakhand.

1. Free Booking Cancellation

We understand travel plans can change due to weather or flight schedules. You can cancel or modify your booking request free of charge up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled vehicle pickup time by informing us via WhatsApp on +91 8923665501.

2. Rescheduling Policy

Date rescheduling is free of charge subject to vehicle availability for your updated dates. If the same vehicle is not available, we will offer an alternative model from our fleet.

3. Security Deposit Refunds

The security deposit is strictly 100% refundable upon vehicle drop-off after basic verification of vehicle condition, fuel parity, and Fastag toll expenses. Refunds are processed immediately via UPI / Cash / Online transfer.

4. Early Returns

In case of early vehicle return, rental charges will be calculated on the actual days utilized (subject to the 1-day minimum rental policy).
