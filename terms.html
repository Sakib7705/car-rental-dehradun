Rental Terms & Guidelines

Clear, transparent rules for renting a self-drive car in Dehradun & Uttarakhand.

1. Documents Required

Before vehicle handover, the customer must present:

  • Original Valid Driving License: Must be held for at least 1 year. (Commercial transport license or valid LMV).
  • Original Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card or Passport (Voter ID accepted for domestic tourists).
  • Customer must be at least 21 years of age.

2. Daily Kilometre Limit (200 KM / Day)

All standard self-drive bookings come with a generous allowance of 200 KM per calendar day. If total kilometres traveled exceed the allowed cumulative limit (e.g. 600 KM for a 3-day rental), extra kilometres will be billed at the standard vehicle rate (ranging from ₹11 to ₹16/km as specified on the vehicle card).

3. Security Deposit & Refund

A refundable security deposit (₹3,000 to ₹5,000 depending on vehicle class) is collected at delivery via UPI, Cash, or Card. This deposit is promptly refunded back upon safe return of the vehicle after verifying fuel level, Fastag/toll clearance, and basic physical check.

4. Fuel & Toll Policy

  • Fuel: All rentals are dry (fuel not included). Vehicles are provided with a working fuel level and must be returned with the same amount.
  • Toll & Fastag: Fastag charges incurred during the trip will be settled at actuals during vehicle drop-off.
  • State Permits: All our cars have valid commercial permits for driving anywhere within Uttarakhand.

5. Prohibited Uses

  • Sub-leasing or handing over driving control to an unauthorized person.
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or intoxicating substances.
  • Participating in vehicle rallies, racing, or illegal commercial activities.
  • Smoking inside the vehicle (deep cleaning penalty applies).
