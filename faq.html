Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about car bookings, pricing, 200 KM limit, and delivery.

You need a valid original Indian Driving License (held for at least 1 year) and an original Aadhaar Card or Passport for identity verification. For international tourists, a valid passport and International Driving Permit (IDP) are required.
Every vehicle comes with a generous allowance of 200 KM per calendar day. The allowance is cumulative: for example, a 3-day booking gives you a total of 600 KM to use flexibly throughout your trip. Any extra kilometres beyond this total are billed at the per-car extra KM rate (₹11 to ₹16/km).
Pricing is calculated per calendar day based on your selected pickup and drop dates. For example, if you rent a Maruti Swift at ₹2,000/day for 3 days, the rental charge is ₹6,000. A refundable security deposit (₹3,000) is held separately and refunded upon return.
The security deposit (₹3,000 to ₹5,000) is collected at vehicle handover. It is refunded 100% immediately via UPI or bank transfer upon return of the car after basic condition check and toll/Fastag reconciliation.
Yes! We offer doorstep delivery across Dehradun including Jolly Grant Airport (DED), Dehradun Railway Station, ISBT, Rajpur Road, as well as locations in Mussoorie and Rishikesh.
